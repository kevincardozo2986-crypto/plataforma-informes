# Recurso de Tomy

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. Referencia: imagen del perro adjuntada por el usuario.
Recurso: app/ui/assets/tomy-badge.png.

Prompt utilizado:

Create a production-ready app mascot badge based very closely on the attached dog reference: friendly fluffy black and white puppy face with green eyes, happy open mouth, floppy ears, 3D animated rendering, circular royal blue badge with subtle blue sun rays and a thin gold rim. Preserve the face and composition of the reference. Single centered circular badge filling 94% of square canvas, actual transparent alpha background outside circle, clean smooth edges no white remnants, no letters no text. This is Tomy for a university desktop application. Save generated image as an asset.

El resultado tiene fondo opaco. La interfaz lo presenta mediante un visor circular que excluye el exterior de la insignia.
